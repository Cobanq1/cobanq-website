About us

An award-winning software provider to the money remittance business

The FX Soft is a technology provider to the remittance industry. Founded in 2014 and based in London, we take pride in helping organisations of every size — banks, money transfer companies, finance institutions and start-ups — with software built around how they actually work.

We started out to build a more transparent platform: one that lets a company serve its customers properly while staying on top of its back-end accounting. Since then we have helped firms expand their networks, and built a wide network of integrated partners you can plug into so you can start trading sooner.
