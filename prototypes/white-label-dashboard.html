White Label Configuration

Run CoBanq online banking on your own domain, under your own brand, for your own customers.

Scope — this whole section is the new build. Four setup steps (right-hand rail) plus a customer-pricing step. Each step saves independently; the rail tick turns green once the step is complete so the tenant can see what is still outstanding.

Domain setup

Behaviour — tenant enters a subdomain plus the root domain they own. On Save & generate records the backend provisions the tenant, returns the DNS record set below, and starts polling for verification. The domain field locks once verification succeeds — changing it afterwards requires Reset domain.

Custom menu links

Extra items appended to the bottom of your customers' sidebar — your support desk, help centre, terms, or any internal tool.

Behaviour — up to 8 links. Each has a label, an absolute URL (https only), an icon from a fixed set, "open in new tab", and a visibility toggle so a link can be parked without deleting it. Order is drag-and-drop in production; arrows are fine as a first cut. Links render below the standard menu, never above it.

Customer pricing

Your margin sits on top of the CoBanq wholesale rate

The left column is what CoBanq charges you. The right column is what your customers are charged in your banking app. The difference is settled to your commission account monthly.
Phase 2 — included so the shape of the screen is agreed early. Validation: the customer price can never be below the wholesale cost; show the resulting margin live; changes need a confirmation step because they affect live customers.
